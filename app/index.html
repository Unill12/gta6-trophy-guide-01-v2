Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

Play in this order and you will not lock yourself out of anything. Each stage lists exactly which trophies it earns; tick them off in the Tracker as you go. Total time for an average player: roughly 90–120 hours, most of it in stages 3–4.

The Platinum itself

Vice Got a Price! pops the moment the other 54 are earned — there is no extra task. The real gate is A Long Road (100% completion), which needs every story mission, stranger strand, activity, collectible set and challenge the game counts. Finish stage 4 before you judge how far off 100% you are: the in-game stats menu shows the exact percentage and what is missing.

Missable trophies — read this before you start

Most of GTA VI can be revisited after the story, but these trophies are tied to moments, choices or one-time setups. “Possibly missable” means the guides disagree or the trigger is still being confirmed — the guide says so instead of guessing. When in doubt, keep a manual save before story choices.

Collectibles & grinds — the long counters

These are the trophies with a number attached. Use the + / − buttons to track your own count; the bar fills as you go. Counts are saved in your browser next to your earned ticks, and travel with the export file.
